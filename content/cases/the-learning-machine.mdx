## THE BRIEF

A training company ran enrolment, instalment payments and assessment tracking across five-to-six spreadsheet systems. Onboarding was slow, tracking was manual, and nothing talked to anything else.

## THE OPERATION

Owned the roadmap and the end-to-end lifecycle: authored the PRD, technical specifications and integration architecture, then shipped the MVP through AI-assisted full-stack development. Wired Stripe payments, Supabase (PostgreSQL), Twilio SMS and Resend email together over REST APIs and webhooks — cutting learner onboarding and automating instalment-payment and assessment tracking.
