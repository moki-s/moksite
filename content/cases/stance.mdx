## THE BRIEF

Three clinics ran scheduling, rosters, patient and doctor management, and billing across separate tools. Operations needed one B2B dashboard instead of many.

## THE OPERATION

Led the end-to-end launch across all three centres: built dynamic views with MongoDB aggregations and Charts, ran Postman API testing and Excel-based data validation, and managed bug triage and prioritisation in JIRA.
