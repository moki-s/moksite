## THE BRIEF

Clinicians at a rehabilitation-tech startup were losing twenty minutes per note to manual data entry — time that belonged with patients, not paperwork.

## THE OPERATION

Proposed and piloted a voice-to-structured-form tool end to end, pairing Google Speech-to-Text with an LLM to turn dictation into clean, structured clinical records. Documentation time fell from twenty minutes to five per note, and the clinicians adopted it.
