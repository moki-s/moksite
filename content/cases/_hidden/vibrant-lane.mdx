## THE BRIEF

A worldwide store selling AI-generated custom wallpaper — a thin launch and a wide-open ceiling, if the art and the unit economics could be made to work at the same time.

## THE OPERATION

Launched and scaled the store: engineered prompts across five-to-seven art styles and optimised the AI token cost per generation so the margins held as volume grew. Revenue scaled roughly tenfold within two months, and the payment gateway was integrated end to end.
