## THE BRIEF

Sales agents were stitching lead intelligence together by hand across disconnected systems. The closers needed one screen, not five tabs.

## THE OPERATION

Built an in-house CRM that integrates Zoho (via the Model Context Protocol) with advertising-platform data, defining the data model that surfaces lead intelligence to the floor. Added a companion tool that issues configurable-deposit payment links and auto-calculates commissions.
