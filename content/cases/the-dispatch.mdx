## THE BRIEF

The brands needed to send marketing at volume — tens of thousands of emails — without wrecking sender reputation or hand-building every template.

## THE OPERATION

Delivered an email engine on Amazon SES sending 10,000+ marketing emails a day across brands. Specced auto-generated templates and brand colours pulled from a site URL, built lifecycle automations — welcome, enrolment, deposit and win-back — and led QA, watching bounce, complaint and deliverability rates per mailbox provider against reputation thresholds behind a warm-up daily cap. Delivered rate held around 99%.
